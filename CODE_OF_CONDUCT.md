# Code of Conduct — TDOP

**Project:** Tanzania Digital Opportunity Platform (TDOP)
**Repository:** https://github.com/Tanzanian-Opportunities/TDOP-docs

## 1. Our commitment

TDOP connects opportunity seekers with trusted opportunities and organizations.
That mission depends on trust, transparency, and fairness — which start with how we
treat each other. We commit to making participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
sex, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, ethnicity, race, nationality,
caste, religion, language, education, socioeconomic status, veteran status, or
experience level.

We do not tolerate harassment or discrimination of project participants in any form,
in person, in written communication, in code review, in issue discussions, in pull
requests, in chat channels, or in public representation of the project.

## 2. Expected behavior

All project participants are expected to:

- **Communicate respectfully.** Assume good intent, and ask clarifying questions before
  escalating. Disagreement about technical decisions is normal; personal attacks are not.
- **Be inclusive.** Welcome newcomers, use welcoming and inclusive language, respect
  different viewpoints and experience, and accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- **Be professional.** Keep discussions relevant to the work, give credit where it is
  due, and focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- **Be accountable.** If you cause harm, acknowledge it, apologize, and correct your
  behavior.
- **Protect privacy.** Do not share other people's personal data, private
  communications, or unpublished security information.
- **Follow project process.** Use the documented workflow in `CONTRIBUTING.md` and
  `DEVELOPMENT_GUIDE.md`, and respect review and merge decisions.

## 3. Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes, but is not limited to:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwanted sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment, stalking, doxxing, or publication of private
  information without consent.
- Discrimination or exclusion based on any characteristic listed in section 1.
- Deliberate misgendering or use of preferred names/nicknames in a mocking way.
- Publishing others' source code, credentials, or private data without permission.
- Intentionally derailing discussions, gatekeeping, or sustained disruption of
  meetings, reviews, or issue threads.
- Reporting conduct issues maliciously, or knowingly making false reports.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## 4. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- the repository, issues, pull requests, and discussions;
- any official project chat, mailing list, or meeting;
- public representation of the project (demos, social media posts made in the name of
  TDOP).

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public
spaces.

## 5. Reporting a violation

If you experience or witness a violation of this Code of Conduct:

1. **Do not confront the person publicly first** if doing so would put you at risk.
2. Report it privately to the project maintainer:
   - Repository maintainer profile: https://github.com/felix202422
   - Private contact: **[TBD — dedicated conduct contact address to be added by the
     project owner]**
3. Include, if possible:
   - what happened, with dates/times and links (issue/PR/commit URLs);
   - any supporting evidence (screenshots, messages);
   - whether you are willing to be named in follow-up.

**All reports will be treated as confidential.** Retaliation against a person who
reports a violation in good faith is itself a violation of this Code of Conduct.

> Security vulnerabilities must **not** be reported through public issues. Follow the
> process in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

## 6. Enforcement and response

The project maintainer (repository owner) is responsible for enforcement. Depending on
severity, enforcement actions may include:

| Level | Examples of response |
|---|---|
| Clarification | Private reminder and a request to correct the behavior |
| Warning | Formal written warning; a temporary restriction on project spaces |
| Temporary restriction | Temporary ban from issues, PRs, or project spaces for a defined period |
| Permanent ban | Permanent exclusion from the project |
| Reversal | Reverting commits, closing PRs, or removing content produced through misconduct |

Severity is assessed on a case-by-case basis, considering: intent, impact on the
affected person(s), prior behavior, and whether the behavior is repeated.

The maintainer will:

- acknowledge receipt of a report within **5 business days**;
- investigate and decide on a response within **15 business days**, or explain why more
  time is needed;
- inform the reporter of the outcome (subject to privacy constraints).

If the maintainer is the subject of a report, the report should be escalated to
**[TBD — alternate/owner-of-record contact]**, and the maintainer must recuse
themselves from the matter.
